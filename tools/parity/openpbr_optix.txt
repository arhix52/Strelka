OpenPBR on OptiX -- first measurement, 2026-09-03
=================================================

CUDA 13.3 / OptiX 9.1 / RTX 4090, at each scene's own sample count.
The `recorded` column in both tables is the Metal figure the harnesses carry;
it was taken before OptiX had OpenPBR at all, so this is the first time the two
backends can be compared on this model.

Read the RATIO column. `rel` is noise and bias in one number and rises as the
sample count falls; the ratio is a brightness offset and barely moves.

The ratio is closer to 1.000 on OptiX than Metal recorded on almost every row --
00_calibration 0.999 against 0.975, 02_basecolor 1.000 against 0.980,
27_ies 1.000 against 0.969. The rows where the two models genuinely differ keep
their Metal values (15_clearcoat 0.917/0.892, 17_coated_glass 0.925/0.903,
18_bounded_volume 0.902/0.905, 23_diffuse_transmission 0.939/0.935), so this is
not the two models collapsing into one: the differences that should be there are
there, and a roughly 2.5% offset that should not be is absent.

25_subsurface is the largest move: 0.024 / 1.000, against 0.203 / 1.182 on
Metal. Checked rather than assumed, because "the models agree" and "the walk
never runs" look alike from a ratio. Rendering it with subsurface_iterations 0
and 64 gives rel 0.2525 and a 3.1% mean difference, so the OpenPBR interior is
entered and walked.

29..32 (the pool caustics rows) have no recorded value -- they were added after
the Metal table was taken. Recorded here for the same reason the rest are.
